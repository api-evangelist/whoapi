If someone asked you to describe a phishing attack, there’s a good chance you would imagine a sophisticated cybercriminal, advanced malware, and a carefully planned operation designed to bypass security systems. The reality is often much less dramatic. Many successful phishing campaigns begin with something surprisingly simple: a newly registered domain.
